Industry Tech Training & Engineering Internships near Katol Road, Nagpur

Katol Road is renowned in Central India as the home of Shri Ramdeobaba College of Engineering and Management (RCOEM / Ramdeobaba University), alongside prominent polytechnic and research centers. Students from Katol Road, Gittikhadan, and Friends Colony travel to our Ramdaspeth institute for advanced hands-on training in Generative AI, Full Stack .NET, and Data Analytics. The clear transit route through Sadar and Sitabuldi makes our Ramdaspeth center the top choice for RCOEM students aiming for top-tier off-campus and product company placements.

Commute Guide

How to Reach Our Ramdaspeth Center from Katol Road

Student-tested transit directions to reach our Ramdaspeth center without traffic delays.

Nagpur Metro Route

Board Orange Line Metro at Kasturchand Park or Chhatrapati Square link, or take a quick 15-minute commute via Sadar / VIP Road down to Ramdaspeth.

Aapli Bus & Public Transit

Direct Aapli Bus service connecting Katol Road Naka via Sadar / Residency Road to Sitabuldi and Central Bazar Road.

Two-Wheeler & Driving

Head south on Katol Road through Friends Colony, cross Gittikhadan, head through Sadar / Civil Lines, and merge onto Central Bazar Road to Ramdaspeth.

Pro Tip:Traveling via Civil Lines (Palm Road) avoids the market crowd in Sadar and brings you straight to Ramdaspeth in 16 to 18 minutes.
