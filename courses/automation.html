Week-by-Week Syllabus — Automation

Module 1Automation Fundamentals & n8n

Week 1
  • What is workflow automation: triggers, actions, conditions, and data transformation

  • Scenario scheduling and trigger types: instant webhooks vs scheduled runs

Frequently Asked Questions — Automation

Is automation a good career in 2026?
Automation skills are in high demand across every industry. Every company — from small businesses to large enterprises — is looking to reduce manual work and increase efficiency. Automation engineers, no-code developers, and RPA specialists are hired by IT services, BPOs, BFSI, and e-commerce companies. The ROI of automation is directly measurable, making it easy to demonstrate value in interviews.
What is the fee for the Automation course in Nagpur?
Fees are shared during a personal advisor call. EMI options and batch-specific pricing are available. Connect via WhatsApp or the enquiry form — an advisor will share the complete fee structure within one business day.
Do I need coding experience to take the Automation course?
No. Weeks 1–3 and 5 use no-code tools (n8n, Make, Zapier, Apps Script) that require no programming. Week 4 introduces Python scripting — if you have never coded, the basics are introduced from scratch. A willingness to follow logic and experiment is more important than prior coding experience.
What is n8n and why is it taught in this course?
n8n is an open-source workflow automation platform similar to Zapier and Make but self-hostable. It allows you to build unlimited workflows without per-task fees — making it ideal for high-volume automations. It is taught first because its visual interface is excellent for learning automation concepts, and self-hosting gives you full control over your data and integrations.
What is the difference between n8n, Make, and Zapier?
Zapier is the most user-friendly but has per-task pricing that gets expensive at scale. Make is more powerful with better data transformation and a more visual interface. n8n is open-source, self-hostable, and has no per-execution limits — ideal for technical users and high-volume workflows. This course teaches all three so you can choose the right tool for each client or project.
Is Google Apps Script covered in this course?
Yes. Week 5 is dedicated to Google Apps Script — automating Google Sheets, Forms, Gmail, and Calendar using JavaScript-based scripting inside Google Workspace. Apps Script triggers (on-form-submit, on-edit, time-based) are covered, and you build a complete CRM → Sheets → email pipeline using it.
Is this Automation course available offline in Nagpur?
Yes. Offline classes at our Wardha Road centre in Nagpur. Online live Zoom and hybrid options also available. Weekend batches run Saturday–Sunday for working professionals.
Can I use automation skills to freelance for local Nagpur businesses?
Yes. Nagpur has a large number of SMEs — medical clinics, retail, manufacturing, education — that are doing manual data entry and follow-ups daily. After this course, you can offer to automate their WhatsApp lead handling, CRM data entry, and reporting workflows. Automation freelancers typically charge per project or as a monthly retainer.
Is Agentic AI different from the Automation course?
Yes. This Automation course focuses on deterministic workflow automation — if X then Y, connecting apps via APIs and webhooks. The Agentic AI course focuses on AI-powered autonomous systems that use LLMs to make decisions and take actions. They are complementary: automation for rule-based processes, agentic AI for tasks requiring reasoning and judgment.
